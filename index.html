BrainAI

BrainAI is a personal, single-user tool. It builds a private, searchable index of its owner's own Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive and Google Contacts so the owner can search their own information.

It runs only on the owner's own computer. It is not a public service, and it does not access anyone else's account.

Google access is read-only. BrainAI never sends, edits or deletes email, events, files or contacts.
